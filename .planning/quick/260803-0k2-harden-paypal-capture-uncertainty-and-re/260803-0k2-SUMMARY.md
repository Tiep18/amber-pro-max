# Quick Task 260803-0k2 Summary

## Outcome

Hardened the PayPal post-approval path so an ambiguous capture can no longer be reported as a definitive failure or expose an immediate second-payment action. Provider reconciliation mismatches now enter a durable `review_required` transition and use customer copy distinct from a verified late payment.

## Changes

- Treat rejected and non-success capture transports as financially uncertain, then reconcile through a PayPal order lookup.
- Keep missing or incomplete capture facts in `verifying` without releasing the inventory hold.
- Persist hard merchant, order, amount, or currency mismatches as `review_required` with sanitized audit facts.
- Distinguish reconciliation review, verified late-payment review, and uncertain capture outcomes in the UI.
- Hide the PayPal button while money may already have moved and remove unsupported no-charge or email promises.
- Add regression coverage for capture exceptions, lookup recovery, unresolved uncertainty, incomplete facts, and hard reconciliation mismatches.
