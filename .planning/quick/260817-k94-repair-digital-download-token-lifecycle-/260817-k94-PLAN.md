Repair the digital-download capability lifecycle end to end without introducing another issuer or weakening customer-safe failure behavior.

Output: One forward migration, regenerated Supabase types, canonical application adapters/actions, and strict RED-GREEN-REFACTOR coverage at database, unit, route, and security boundaries.
</objective>
